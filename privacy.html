Block Rush Privacy Policy

App: Block Rush (App Store name "Block Rush Online") · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Block Rush is a multiplayer game where you memorize a block shape and rebuild it. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it to the minimum the game needs to work.

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking

  • You don't create an account with an email or password. The game creates an anonymous account for your device automatically, so your games and wins are kept.
  • The game shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information.

Information the game uses

  • Anonymous player ID: a random identifier created for your device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID. On iPhone it is stored in the device's secure Keychain so it survives reinstalling the app.
  • Display name: the name you type on the title screen. Other players in your room see it, and it appears on the leaderboard if you win online matches. Please don't use your full real name.
  • Game data: your games left each day, matches you played, online wins (for the leaderboards and monthly prizes), promo codes you redeemed, and your device's time zone (so free games reset at your midnight).
  • Purchases: purchases are made through Apple. We receive a confirmation of what you bought so we can unlock it. We never see your payment card or Apple ID details.
  • Gameplay during a match: your blocks, scores and the quick-chat phrases you pick are sent to the other players in your room while you play. They are not saved after the room closes. There is no free-text chat.

How the information is used

  • To run matches between players and show rooms that are open.
  • To keep your daily free games, purchases, promo codes, wins and leaderboard rank.
  • To give monthly prizes to the top players.
  • To keep the game fair and working (for example, checking the app version).

Where it is stored

Game data is stored on our game server, provided by Supabase (supabase.com), which hosts it on our behalf. Purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We use these providers only to run the game.

How long we keep it

We keep your game data while you play. Leaderboards keep wins for the current day and month. If you ask us to delete your data, we delete your anonymous account and everything linked to it.

Children

Block Rush is suitable for all ages. We do not ask for personal information such as email, phone number or location, and there is no free-text chat. If you believe a child has entered personal information as their display name, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • You can change your display name at any time on the title screen.
  • You can ask us to delete your game data by contacting us (include your display name and roughly when you played).
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
